What are TABS?

Terp Account Billboards are on-chain identities that map human-readable names to Terp Network addresses. Once registered, a name permanently resolves to your wallet.

Pricing

Cost is calculated from the length of the name you register. Shorter names require a higher premium. The price components are derived from the contract's configured tier rates.

10+ chars --

Rules

  • Only lowercase alphanumeric characters and hyphens
  • Must start with a letter
  • Cannot end with a hyphen
  • Minimum 1 character, maximum 64 characters
  • Names are permanent — no transfers or releases

Resolution

Registered names resolve on-chain. Any dApp integrated with the TABS registry can look up your-name and retrieve the owning address.
